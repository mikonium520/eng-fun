Ellie's Exam Review Notes

🌟 Positive Friends & Behaviours

Adjectives: caring, generous, good-tempered, supportive, considerate, polite, easygoing, humorous, patient, honest/trustworthy.

Good Friends: listen to, talk to, look after, share with, cheer up, stand up for, count on.

⚠️ Negative Friends & Behaviours

Adjectives: naughty, rude, dishonest, greedy, selfish, impatient, mean, cranky, bad-tempered, gloomy, hot-headed.

Don't: fight with, argue with, shout at, laugh at, play tricks on, pick on, make fun of, show off to.

📝 Past Tense Verbs (Regular Rules)

  • General: add -ed (ask $\rightarrow$ asked, cheer $\rightarrow$ cheered)
  • Ends in 'e': add -d (use $\rightarrow$ used, smile $\rightarrow$ smiled, bake $\rightarrow$ baked)
  • Short vowel + consonant: double last letter + ed (stop $\rightarrow$ stopped, hop $\rightarrow$ hopped, hug $\rightarrow$ hugged)
  • Consonant + y: remove y + add -ied (cry $\rightarrow$ cried, try $\rightarrow$ tried, carry $\rightarrow$ carried)
  • Vowel + y: keep y + add -ed (play $\rightarrow$ played, stay $\rightarrow$ stayed)
  • Auxiliaries: is/am $\rightarrow$ was, are $\rightarrow$ were, can $\rightarrow$ could

💡 Advice & classroom Prepositions

have to / should / shouldn't + Base Verb: "You should share toys." / "You shouldn't throw a tantrum."

Prepositions: by the wall, by the window, at the front of, at the back of, in the middle of, between A and B, behind, on my right/left.
